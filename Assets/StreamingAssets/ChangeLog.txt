Changelog: Anatomy & Combat Overhaul
Major Features Added

    Added Anatomy System: Characters now have a detailed anatomical structure.
        Body parts can take damage, lose functionality, or be destroyed.
        Vital body parts (e.g., Head, Torso) cause death if lost.
        Limb loss affects combat (e.g., losing a hand means dropping weapons).

NPC & Character Generation

    NPCs now generate with the correct anatomy based on their Race and BodyType.
    AnatomyGenerator now correctly assigns anatomy to characters upon creation.
    NPCGenerator ensures generated NPCs receive matching anatomy.

Combat System Overhaul

    Damage now targets individual body parts instead of just overall health.
    Lost limbs affect gameplay (e.g., dropping weapons when a hand is lost).
    Vital part destruction results in instant death (e.g., decapitation).
    Resistance now applies per body part, affecting damage mitigation.
    Critical hits increase damage and can destroy body parts outright.

Combat Messaging Improvements

    Added detailed combat messages explaining exactly what happens:
        "Orc strikes Knight's Right Arm for 15 damage!"
        "Knight has lost their Right Arm!"
        "Knight drops Longsword!"
        "Knight has been fatally wounded and dies!"
    Messages now log to both the console and the in-game combat log.
    Ensures clearer battle feedback for players.

Inventory & Item Handling

    Weapons, shields, and gear are unequipped and dropped when a body part is lost.
    Fixed duplication issue—items are now removed from the character before being dropped.
    Dropped items correctly appear on the ground and can be picked up by others.

Code Clean-Up & Optimizations

    Removed redundant inventory management from NPC.cs (now handled in Character.cs).
    Fixed TakeDamage() to distribute damage correctly to body parts.
    Updated CanEquipItem() to prevent equipping items on non-functional limbs.
    Fixed Enum.TryParse() issue for BodyPosition parsing in AnatomyDataLoader.cs.
    Refactored DropItem() to properly remove items before adding them to the world.
    Ensured GetRandomBodyPart() selects only valid, existing body parts.

Summary

    Added Anatomy system with destructible body parts.
    NPCs now spawn with appropriate anatomy based on Race.
    Combat now distributes damage across body parts, with realistic consequences.
    Items are properly unequipped and dropped when losing limbs.
    Combat messages now provide detailed explanations of attacks, injuries, and deaths.
    Fixed multiple bugs and optimized existing mechanics.
	
	

Changelog: Underground & Cave System Overhaul
Major Features Added
Underground Water System

    Introduced a WaterLevel system (0-100) to track underground moisture.
    Cells now have subterranean water based on their surface proximity to water bodies.
    Added underground water pockets, which can be used for wells or aquifers in the future.
    SubterraneanTerrain now correctly reflects underground water presence, not just WaterLevel.

Cave System Revamp

    Caves are now generated in the subterranean layer instead of directly on the surface.
    SubterraneanTerrain = TerrainType.Cave now correctly marks cave areas underground.
    Caves now expand using procedural generation, ensuring organic cave networks.
    Introduced cave connections, forming large underground tunnel networks.
    Cave entrances are now separate from caves, allowing for surface-to-underground entry points.

Map & Terrain Adjustments

    The GenerateSubterraneanWaterMap() function now sets underground water correctly based on WaterLevel.
    Adjusted WaterLevel expansion to ensure water spreads naturally underground.
    Underground lakes can now form in areas with high WaterLevel.

Cave & NPC Generation Improvements

    CaveGenerator was updated to integrate procedural cave expansion while preserving old functionality.
    Caves now generate with randomized cave types, including:
        Treasure Caves
        Monster Lairs
        Fungi Caves
        Abandoned Camps
        Collapsed Tunnels
    Cave IDs are now assigned consistently across all connected tiles, ensuring caves remain unified.

Ore Generation System

    Introduced ResourceTags for tracking underground Iron, Copper, Silver, Gold, Mithril, and Adamantine.
    Ores now spawn based on terrain type, with:
        Common metals (Iron, Copper) appearing in mountains and underground areas.
        Rare ores (Gold, Mithril, Adamantine) found in deep caves or hidden veins.
    Ores now use random distribution, ensuring each world generates uniquely.

Bug Fixes & Code Optimizations

    Fixed an issue where Caves spawned in Mountain Peaks, making them inaccessible.
    Fixed an issue where subterranean water was not appearing correctly despite WaterLevel.
    Fixed IsPositionValid not being accessible in CaveGenerator by using MapGenerator.Instance.
    Optimized cave and water expansion algorithms for better performance on large maps.
    Removed redundancy by merging old CaveGenerator functionality with new procedural cave expansion.

Summary

    Caves now exist fully underground instead of being part of the surface terrain.
    WaterLevel now properly influences underground water, creating subterranean lakes and moisture zones.
    Ore deposits now generate in the underground layer, tied to specific terrain features.
    Caves are procedurally generated, forming connected networks rather than isolated cells.
    Surface cave entrances now allow access to underground tunnels.
    Codebase optimized, with redundant checks and outdated methods removed.
	
	

Changelog: Civilisation & Village Expansion Overhaul
Major Features Added

Village & Civilization Expansion

    Villages now expand their territory organically, prioritizing high-value land.
    Expansion decisions consider population growth, available resources, prestige level, and defense needs.
    Dynamic cell scoring system evaluates terrain desirability before expansion.
    Expansion Points introduced, which dictate how much land a village can claim based on its prosperity.

Civilization-Level Growth

    Civilizations can now claim large regions, influencing multiple villages.
    When a civilization's influence grows, it can establish new villages.
    Border conflicts may arise when civilizations push against each other.

Adaptive Village Growth

    Villages now respond dynamically to nearby resources:
        Expansion prioritizes fertile land for farming villages.
        Mining towns seek out mountains and stone-rich areas.
        Trade hubs expand along roads and rivers.
    Special landmarks (rivers, ancient ruins, sacred sites) now influence village growth.

Village AI Improvements

    Villages intelligently choose expansion paths rather than growing randomly.
    A village may halt expansion if food, water, or wood supplies are too low.
    Defensive villages may prioritize fortified positions over farmland.

New Civilization Expansion Mechanics

    Factions now influence expansion: A highly aggressive civilization may rapidly seize land.
    Different races have unique expansion preferences (e.g., Elves prefer forests, Dwarves seek mountains).
    New diplomatic events triggered by territorial disputes.

Debugging & Optimizations

    GameDebugger & MessageLogManager now log all village expansions.
    Expansion logic rewritten for better readability and maintainability.
    Ensured compliance with coding rules (avoiding redundant code, improving list storage efficiency).

Summary

    Villages expand more intelligently, with resource-driven growth.
    Civilizations now claim land dynamically and found new settlements.
    Expansion mechanics consider faction traits, diplomacy, and environmental factors.
    Added debugging and performance optimizations for better tracking and scaling.
